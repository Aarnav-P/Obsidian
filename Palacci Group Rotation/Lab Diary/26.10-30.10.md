#### Day Log
Starting at 11AM because of these awful bus delays! No time >:L

Overall workflow should look like:
1. Calibrate camera: get K, D
2. For one frame, detect ArUco markers
3. Undistort marker positions / frame
4. Compute homography: undistorted image pixels -> floor coords
5. For every video frame:
   a. undistort frame, or undistort tracked point coordinates
   b. track object in image coordinates
   c. map tracked object point through $H$ (homography matrix)\[H_img_to_floor\]
   d. get floor position in metres
   e. compute velocity from floor positions over time

Homography seems to work well:
![[Pasted image 20261028140616.png]]

We measured out that with the 3x3cm blocks that the markers were spaced as 1.4575m in $x$ and 1.2305m in $y$ giving a ratio of $x/y=$ 1.1844.

Measuring pixels on the `top_down_floor_view` gives us 1456x1234 which gives $x/y=$ 1.1799 and a pixel mapping of 1 px = 1.001 cm in $x$ and 0.9972cm in y.

The A4 paper is measured at 29.6cm in height by FIJI and the Hexbug at about 4.2cm.

Which, given that the camera calibration is not actually right (i bumped it) and the measurements on the ground are not the most precise in the world, is quite good. 

-- the plan from here is to work on the tracking algorithm leaving things suboptimal because it is small enough to not matter for getting $l_p$ .
