I emailed Gerhard Jung from VSMD 26 and he replied quickly with a [[Swarmodroid_and_AMPy_Reconfigurable_Bristle-Bots_amp_Software_Package_for_Robotic_Active_Matter_Studies.pdf|paper]] [1]and the name of the russian group making bristlebots. I want to take some minutes to read this paper actually.

So it linked to a whole bunch more hexbug papers and in the end i read a bunch of them as well. I didn't really find anything interesting though except for a paper showing that two bugs positioned face to face and spaced by an elastic beam can buckle the beam and therefore you have alignment as a function of buckling. I don't think the maze-solving properties there really transfer over though, since in the chains i am making the bots are all aligned.

It does pose the question though, if I can anti align some bots, what may I be able to do? some sort of tunable transition between buckling and oscillating? Something to think about with a clearer head.

As for the [[Swarmodroid_and_AMPy_Reconfigurable_Bristle-Bots_amp_Software_Package_for_Robotic_Active_Matter_Studies.pdf|Swarmodroid Type II]] , once I reached far down enough I saw that it is 1. Twice the length (bigger spaces needed) 2. Five times the weight 3. Twice the price.
