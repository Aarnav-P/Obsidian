#### Day Log
Still kinda sick.
Went to Marton and we got a few different designs sorted out.
My main strategy is to fix the bugs by means of using heavy PLA blocks as clamps at the ends of the chain. In order to do this I have modified the end pieces. Zheng allows her end hexbot to oscillate too so I have also made this change. 
The second change is that we cannot laser cut single pieces, so I have gone for a modular design. The current downside is that by virtue of needing to be connected the pieces are only remotely well attached at the connection points. As a result, the ends of the arms droop and the chain is not very effective. 

To fix this, we can try two things. Currently there is a mortise and tenon arrangement with a rectangular tenon. There is one tenon at the near side of the arm and for the complementary piece a mortise on the far end of the arm. 

1. Make the chain body out of PLA, which snaps together unlike TPU. We are doing this.
2. Change the design of the tenon to be some sort of inverted trapezium, so that it can be pushed through the mortise with TPU and the joint will be secure.
