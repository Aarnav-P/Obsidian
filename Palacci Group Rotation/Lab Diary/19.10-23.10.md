\-- Instead of trying to have some neatly tracked trajectory let me just track a whole bunch of individual trajectories until I lose the tracking and then combine the statistics.

From [1] we know 
	"The hexbug motion is tracked at 25 Hz using a standard CCD camera. The hexbug has a length *L = 45 mm, a characteristic width 15 mm and height 15 mm and a mass m = 7.5 g*, including the battery. Its motion is obtained from an internal vibration (frequency f0 = 115 Hz and acceleration Γ0 ' 1.5g) transmitted to twelve legs, the shape of which ensures propulsion at a typical speed $v_0$ = 100 mm/s. The *persistence length of the motion exceeds one meter*"

Given that $l_p = v\tau_p$  and from some rough measurement I think it's more $v\sim0.2$m/s, and we have some idea that $l_p\sim 1$m, so we ought to want to measure probably on the order of $\sim\tau_p$ to have a reasonable data window for a single run.
