The important thing here is that the width is tunable, the length doesn't matter as long as it gets overlapped by the next bug's frame.

So, Eloi estimates with TPU 95A HF, $\ell =55$mm, that he gets [[Intern Report|(with a factor 2 error?)]]] 0.013 for his elastoactive parameter. I think its probably half that, which means we have work to get to             $\sigma_C=0.16$.

With [[Bambu_TPU_95A_HF_Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf|TPU 95A HF]] we get a Young's Modulus of 9.8±0.7 MPa and with [[Bambu_TPU_85A_Technical_Data_Sheet_a7233668-497b-487f-af8a-3adb49fafaa1.pdf|| TPU 85A]] , which is the softest TPU available, we have E = 6.8 ± 0.7 MPa

**Given that $C = EJ/L$, where $J$ is a torsional constant, this nets us only a 1.44x improvement. To reach $\sigma_C$ we need something on the order of an 10x increase.**

Ok, so this is one source of improvement. Current $L = 7$mm so we can also improve this a bit, but if we extend it too much does the model still work? 

We can also modify $J$. $J = w^3h\left(\dfrac{16}{3} - 3.36\dfrac{2}{h}\left(1-\dfrac{w^4}{12h^4}\right)\right)$ . $h=2$mm but we can print as low as 0.2mm in theory.
